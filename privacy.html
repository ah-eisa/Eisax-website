Privacy Policy

Entity: EISAX FZ-LLC  ·  Jurisdiction: United Arab Emirates  ·  Last updated: September 2026

EISAX FZ-LLC ("EisaX", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to handling personal data carefully and transparently. This policy outlines how we treat information on our corporate website eisax.com, having regard to applicable United Arab Emirates data protection principles, including Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL). This notice reflects our operational practices and general standards of data governance.

1. Scope & Application

This Privacy Policy applies to the official corporate portal eisax.com and related public web properties operated directly by EISAX FZ-LLC. Individual products in our ecosystem (such as WealthGate AI, EISAX Agent, and SaveBuddy AI) may maintain dedicated product-specific privacy disclosures covering account-level data processing.

2. Information We Collect

Our corporate website is designed as a privacy-first, static information portal. We do not require account registration or collect invasive tracking data on this site:

Source Data Elements Purpose & Legal Basis
Direct Communication Name, corporate email, inquiry message To respond to business, investor, or partnership inquiries (Consent & Legitimate Interest).
Edge Delivery & Security IP address, user-agent, request timestamp, HTTP referrer Automated threat mitigation, DDoS prevention, and global content delivery via Cloudflare Pages (Legitimate Interest).
Aggregated Metrics Page requests, geography, device type Anonymous, cookie-less performance monitoring without cross-site profiling.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We do not deploy third-party marketing cookies, behavioral profiling pixels, or cross-site tracking technologies on eisax.com. We use local browser storage (localStorage) solely to store your preferred interface theme (Dark or Light) and selected language (English or Arabic), ensuring a seamless user experience across sessions.

4. Data Retention & Security

We apply robust administrative, physical, and technical controls to safeguard all corporate correspondence and infrastructure:

  • Encryption: All traffic is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 with strict HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • Storage: Business communications are retained only as long as necessary to satisfy the operational purpose or legal requirements.
  • Infrastructure Security: Edge hosting utilizes enterprise-grade isolation, automated DDoS filtering, and Content Security Policies.

5. Your Rights

In accordance with applicable UAE data protection principles and general data governance standards, you may:

  • Request confirmation of whether your personal information is processed.
  • Access, rectify, or request the erasure of your personal data.
  • Restrict or object to specific processing activities.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
